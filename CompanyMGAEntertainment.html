MGA Entertainment

MGA Entertainment was started by Isaac Lazarian

It seems that all MGA products were sold through third party sellers (Giochi Perozi, as one noted instance.) until 2019 where MGA Entertainment started selling to Australia directly. The earliest noted product that MGA directly made was Bratz.

There might've been an earlier MGA product imported into Asutralia but so far, the records are incomplete.
